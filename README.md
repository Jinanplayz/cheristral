What inspired me to make this? And why update the website now?
The first thing I ever learnt was WebDev, that is how my programming journey started. This is my first website that I actually shipped for the whole world to see. A few months ago I launched it on Hostinger as I participated in a "Shark tank"-like competition to advertise a business. Then me and my friends decided to dedicate parts of our week to turn this into a reality. I worked hard on this website but it had a ton of bugs when I moved it from Hostinger which was insanely costly to the free option Cloudflare pages, but a ton of things broke, the site was extremely slow, filled with random files which were never used or needed and none of the images were loading properly, not to mention the severe lack of optimization for phone. So I decided to add my personal CV to it and fix all the issues to make it usable now as our studio starts to work on its first game.



How much was done with AI and how much was my own work?
AI was primarily used in debugging, such as helping me setup things, fix files when they broke, fix issues and help me analyze/compare the best ways to go about doing something. Besides that, I wrote most of the text on the site and the CV was directly from my actual CV that I wrote about a year ago, some text such as the one in the two "upcoming" games section and jobs section were generated by AI but I edited them, and little bit in the about page, these are what I call placeholder text as I updated parts of them over time but they were initially AI-generated. Some features such as forms and service worker cache configs were done by AI as I have zero experience on these. But about 60-70% of the website was my own hard work now and before but I did use Claude mainly as AI-support due to it being my first site that is deployed publicly. Claude was also used to check the code for security vulnerabilities and to make sure I don't leak any private info.



How did I think of improvements?
Simple, I went over the site with my friends, noted down issues, feedback and suggestions. Then I implemented them and fixed the issues.
